A Quick Overview of Abacus AI&#x27;s Multi-Model Platform ChatLLM is an AI workspace developed by Abacus AI that provides access to multiple large language models (LLMs) from one interface.

- Instead of offering access to a single AI model, ChatLLM brings together many of the world&#x27;s leading AI models under one subscription.
- TL;DR ChatLLM is essentially a hub that brings together many of today&#x27;s leading AI models, so you don&#x27;t have to juggle multiple subscriptions.
- Developers will appreciate the coding-focused models and the additional tools that come with Abacus AI Desktop and the coding agent.
- Rather than choosing between ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek, or Qwen, users can access all of them inside a single platform.
