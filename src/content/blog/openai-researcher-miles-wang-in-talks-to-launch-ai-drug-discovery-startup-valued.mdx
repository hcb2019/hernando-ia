## O Que Aconteceu

Fonte: **TechCrunch** — 15 jul 2026

Miles Wang , an OpenAI researcher whose work includes using AI to accelerate scientific and biological discovery, is leaving the ChatGPT maker to launch a new startup focused on developing AI models for drug discovery, according to four people with knowledge of his plans.

Several other OpenAI researchers are expected to join the new company.

Wang is in talks to raise about $200 million at a $2 billion valuation, two of the people said.

- Talks are ongoing, the deal may not be final and details could change.
- Lightspeed is in discussions to lead the funding round, according to sources.
