The company announced MAI-Cyber-1-Flash , a compact security model developed in-house by its Microsoft AI (MAI) division, embedded inside MDASH , Microsoft&#x27;s multi-agent harness for finding and fixing software vulnerabilities.

MAI-Cyber-1-Flash was designed to handle up to 90% of security tasks efficiently, while MDASH escalates the remaining 10% of exceptionally difficult problems to a larger frontier model — which, notably, is OpenAI&#x27;s GPT-5.4 .
