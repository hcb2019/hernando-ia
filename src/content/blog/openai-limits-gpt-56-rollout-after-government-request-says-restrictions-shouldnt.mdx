## O Que Aconteceu

📰 Fonte: **TechCrunch** — 26 jun 2026

OpenAI is limiting the release of its newest AI models to a “small group of trusted partners” at the behest of the U.S.

government, the company said Friday.

The next generation GPT-5.6 lineup includes Sol, its flagship model; Terra, a more balanced model for everyday use; and Luna, a faster, lower-cost option.

- Although Sol is the company’s most powerful model, the Trump administration has restricted the release of all three.
- OpenAI said the preview is limited to partners “whose participation has been shared with the government.”
