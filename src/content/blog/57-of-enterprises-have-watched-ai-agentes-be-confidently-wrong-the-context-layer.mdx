In the past six months, 57% of enterprises traced a confident but wrong AI agent answer to missing or inconsistent business context, and 31% said it happened more than once, according to a VB Pulse June 2026 survey of 101 qualified enterprises with more than 100 employees.

75% don&#x27;t have an agentic context layer yet The context layer is meant to be a shared model of what business data actually means, built once and referenced consistently instead of re-derived by every agent that touches it.

- Among companies already building or running a governed context layer, 78% report a confident-wrong failure — an AI agent that answered with total certainty and was still wrong.
- Agents don&#x27;t just need more tokens or better models.
- Retrieval over documents is the default way agents get business context for 38% of enterprises, nearly double the next closest approach.
- Ease of ingestion and operational simplicity lead the selection criteria, with retrieval accuracy running behind both.
- The accuracy problem only shows up after the system is already live.
