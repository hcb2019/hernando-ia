## O Que Aconteceu

📰 Fonte: **TechCrunch** — 22 jun 2026

For AI chipmaker Groq, the answer appears to be raise more money from investors — who were said to have profited handsomely after a deal with Nvidia in December — hire more talent, and pivot.

What does an AI company do after one of those not-acqui-hire deals, where a rival pays investors a hefty IP &#8220;licensing&#8221; fee while poaching its critical talent?

On Monday, Groq announced a new $650 million funding round, confirming earlier reports .

- The raise comes roughly six months after Nvidia signed a non-exclusive licensing agreement for Groq&#8217;s technology and hired away founder and CEO Jonathan Ross, president Sunny Madra, and other employees.
- It was last valued at $6.9 billion following a $750 million round in September.
- Groq did not disclose its new valuation.
