## The Beginning

Six months ago, I had nothing but an idea and a lot of coffee.

Today, my AI SaaS product crossed R$50,000 in monthly recurring revenue. This is the story of how it happened — the wins, the mistakes, and everything in between.

## Finding the Problem

I didn't start with a solution. I started with a problem: **content creators spend way too much time repurposing content.**

Every creator I talked to had the same workflow:
1. Write a blog post
2. Chop it into tweets
3. Turn it into a LinkedIn post
4. Script a YouTube Short
5. Create an email newsletter version

That's hours of work. Per piece of content.

## Building the MVP

My MVP took 3 weeks. One input: a blog post URL. One output: repurposed content for 5 platforms.

I used:
- **GPT-4** for content transformation
- **Next.js** for the frontend
- **Supabase** for auth and database
- **Stripe** for payments

No fancy architecture. No microservices. Just ship.

## The Growth Playbook

Here's exactly what drove growth:

1. **Product Hunt launch** — Got 200+ upvotes and the first 50 paying users
2. **Content marketing** — Wrote about the problem I was solving
3. **Cold DMs** — Messaged 20 creators per day on Twitter
4. **Built-in virality** — Every output had a "Generated by [product]" watermark

## What I'd Do Differently

- Charge from day one. Free users are not your friends.
- Talk to users every single day. Not surveys — real conversations.
- Ignore SEO for the first 3 months. Word of mouth beats Google.
