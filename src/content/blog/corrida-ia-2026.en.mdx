## Anthropic: From Private to Public in Record Time

**Anthropic** — creator of Claude, one of the world's most advanced AI models — has just confidentially filed for its **IPO**. The news comes just one week after the company announced a **$65 billion** funding round at a **$965 billion** valuation.

> "Training frontier models and serving inference has enormous upfront cost," explained Daniela Amodei, co-founder, at the Bloomberg Tech Conference. "Companies pushing the frontier will need access to capital — and the public market is very well suited for that."

**Why this matters:** Anthropic is signaling that the AI race isn't slowing down anytime soon. Training the next Claude requires ever-larger data centers, more GPUs, more energy. The private market is already saturated with investment — the exit is going public.

---

## Airbnb Wants Its Piece Too

It's not just Anthropic betting big. **Brian Chesky**, Airbnb CEO, announced plans to launch his **own AI lab**.

The news, confirmed by Bloomberg and TechCrunch, places Chesky in the select group of Silicon Valley executives dissatisfied with the models available on the market. Although Airbnb already uses AI-powered coding tools, Chesky said last year that he still hadn't partnered with any major LLM lab because the products "simply weren't ready."

Now, instead of waiting, he's going to build.

---

## What This Means for 2026

Two moves that reveal the same truth:

1. **Frontier AI is absurdly expensive.** $65 billion isn't enough — Anthropic needs the public market.
2. **No one wants to depend on a single supplier.** Airbnb wants its own lab. Other companies will follow.
3. **The competition is no longer between OpenAI and Google.** It's between everyone who has capital, data, and talent.

For us, engineers and entrepreneurs, the lesson is clear: AI infrastructure is becoming as fundamental as electricity. Knowing how to build **with** and **on top of** these models is the most valuable skill of 2026.
