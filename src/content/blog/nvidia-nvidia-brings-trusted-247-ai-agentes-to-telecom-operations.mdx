Telecom operators have seen remarkable returns from using generative AI to automate network management, customer care and back-office operations.

Most of that impact has been task‑based: automation that speeds up predetermined steps while people manually correlate insights and direct next steps.

Automation is no longer the finish line — it’s the launchpad to autonomy.
